# Shock Cap v2

## Description

Replacement shock caps for Traxxas RC cars. Variants cover the standard cap, a version with a drainage hole, a multi-cap print layout, and a companion pressure fit ring. The STEP source file is included for further CAD editing.

## Printing notes

TPU or flexible filament recommended for the caps to maintain a proper seal against the shock body. The pressure fit ring may need ±0.1–0.2 mm tolerance tuning depending on your printer and filament shrinkage.

## Assembly / use notes

Press cap firmly onto the shock body. The pressure fit ring seats inside or around the cap neck depending on variant — test fit before final assembly.
