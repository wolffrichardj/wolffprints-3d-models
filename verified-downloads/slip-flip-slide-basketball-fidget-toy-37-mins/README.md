# Slip Flip Slide Basketball Fidget Toy (37 mins)

## Description

Let this fully cool after print (you can remove from the build plate) and rotate the hinges – NOT the pin – and then press the pin between your fingers to break the supports (don’t twist the pin – see image below). If you have filament and settings dialed in you can also gently twist the pin until you feel a little resistance, then twist the other way gently until it fully loosens.
