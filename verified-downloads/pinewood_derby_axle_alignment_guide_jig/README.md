# Pinewood Derby Axle Alignment Guide / Jig

## Description

&lt;p&gt;Building a Pinewood Derby car? Getting the axles (nails) inserted perfectly straight is one of the hardest parts of the build. If the axle goes in at a downward or upward angle, the car will rock or wobble.&lt;br&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;I designed this simple alignment jig to solve that problem. It slots directly into the pre-cut axle groove on the wooden block, guiding your axle insertion. It ensures your wheels sit flat and your car rolls straight!&lt;/p&gt;&lt;h2&gt;How to Use&lt;/h2&gt;&lt;ol&gt;&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Position the Jig:&lt;/strong&gt; Insert the small rectangular tab on the 3D printed part into the pre-cut axle slot on your wooden car block.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;&...
