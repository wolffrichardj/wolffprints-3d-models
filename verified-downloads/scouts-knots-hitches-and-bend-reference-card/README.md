# Scouts Knots Hitches and Bend Reference Card

## Description

Print this reference card and clip it to your gear when you need a quick reference while you are scouting or exploring the wilderness with a rope. There are 7 common knots: Bowline Knot (Secure Loop, easy to undo), Clove Hitch (Tie to a post, adjustable and secure), Square Knot (Reef Knot, join two ropes of equal thickness), Taut-Line Hitch (Adjustable loop for lines under tension), Two Half-Hitches (Secure a rope to a post), Sheet Bend (Join ropes of different thickness), and Figure-Eight Knot (Stopper knot, easy to identify).
