# Collapsible TPU Bottle ● 600ml

## Description

**Collapsible Bottle | Compact &amp; Lightweight 🏕️**This 3D printed collapsible bottle is designed for outdoor use — great for hiking, camping, or tossing in your daypack.
It folds down to less than a third of its full height, making it ultra space-efficient when empty, and expands to hold up to **600 ml** when needed.
